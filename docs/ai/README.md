# AI assistants

[Home](../../README.md) · [Connection help](mcp.md) · [Privacy and sharing](../privacy-and-sharing.md)

Ask a compatible AI assistant about your household's plans, find something you've
saved, or get help adding and updating information in ComingUp. You decide whether
to connect an assistant and what it can access.

## What you can ask

- “Use ComingUp to show our schedule for this weekend.”
- “Find the note about school pickup.”
- “Add milk and apples to our shopping list.”
- “Create a ComingUp event for soccer practice Thursday at 5 pm.”
- “Save this recipe privately in ComingUp.”

An assistant can work with the information you give it permission to use. It can
help create or change events, lists, notes, contacts, and private recipes in
ComingUp. Google and iCloud events can be viewed, but must be edited in their
original calendar app.

Assistants cannot permanently delete your events, lists, notes, recipes, contacts,
or household. They can remove individual list items. They cannot access your
photos, change your subscription, or manage household membership.

## How connection works

ComingUp uses **MCP**, a shared way for AI assistants to connect to other apps.
You don't need to learn commands: follow your assistant's setup guide, sign in
to ComingUp, and choose what access to allow. Then ask questions naturally.

If your assistant asks for a connection address, see [connection help](mcp.md).
Never put your password into the conversation.

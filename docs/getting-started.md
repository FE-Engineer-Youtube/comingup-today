# Getting started

[Home](../README.md) · [Features](features.md) · [Integrations](integrations.md)

## Try it first

Open the [public demo household](https://www.comingup.today/demo) to explore
ComingUp with a made-up family's information. You can get a feel for the app
without seeing anyone's private data. Some features, such as connecting your
own calendars, need an account.

## Set up your household

1. Visit [ComingUp Today](https://www.comingup.today) and create an account.
2. Review [plans and limits](https://www.comingup.today/pricing). The household's
   Owner chooses a paid plan in Billing. Creating an account does not subscribe you.
3. Connect a Google or iCloud account and choose which calendars to show.
4. Add events in ComingUp, save useful contacts, and start your notes, lists,
   or recipes.
5. Open Today, Week, or Month to see your schedules together.

To put the day on a shared screen, open Household Display on a tablet or computer
with a web browser. Ambient mode automatically moves between your household's
information and selected photos. Choose what you want people nearby to see.

Where your browser supports it, you can install ComingUp on your device for
easy access. You still need an internet connection to make changes. You can also
turn on optional event reminders on supported devices.

## Connect an assistant

Want to ask questions about your schedule or add something by talking to an AI
assistant? Start with the [AI assistant guide](ai/README.md). You choose whether
to connect an assistant and what it can access.

Questions? Read the [FAQ](https://www.comingup.today/faq) or email
[support@comingup.today](mailto:support@comingup.today).

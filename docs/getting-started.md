# Getting started

[Home](../README.md) · [Features](features.md) · [Integrations](integrations.md)

## Try it first

Open the [public demo household](https://www.comingup.today/demo) to explore
ComingUp with synthetic household data. It is separate from signed-in households
and does not expose a real family's information. The demo illustrates the product;
it does not exercise every connected-provider or account workflow.

## Set up your household

1. Visit [ComingUp Today](https://www.comingup.today) and create an account.
2. Review [plans and limits](https://www.comingup.today/pricing). An Owner selects
   a paid plan through Billing; creating an account is separate from subscribing.
3. Connect a supported Google or iCloud account and choose which calendars to show.
   Imported events stay owned by their source calendar.
4. Add ComingUp events, people and contacts, notes, lists, or recipes as needed.
5. Open Today, Week, or Month to see your schedules together.

For a shared screen, open Household Display on a browser-capable tablet or
computer. Ambient mode can rotate through available household content, including
selected managed photos. Review what should be visible to people near the screen.

ComingUp is also installable as a progressive web app in supported browsers.
Installation does not imply offline editing. Web push reminders are optional
and depend on browser and device support.

## Connect an assistant

Use the [AI assistant guide](ai/README.md) when you want a compatible assistant to
read household information or make supported ComingUp changes. Connection is
optional and requires signing in and approving permissions.

# Help and feedback

[Home](../README.md) · [AI assistants](ai/README.md) · [FAQ](https://www.comingup.today/faq)

## Get an answer

Start with the [FAQ](https://www.comingup.today/faq) or ask a supported assistant
“How does this work in ComingUp?” It can look up ComingUp's published guides and
explain features, setup, and common questions. Looking up public information does
not require access to your household data, though your assistant may ask you to
connect ComingUp first.

## Report a problem or suggest a feature

With a working ComingUp connection, ask your assistant to help describe the issue
or idea. Review the description, then ask it to submit a report. For example:

- “Help me report that my shopping list isn't updating.”
- “Submit a feature request for this display option.”

You must allow the assistant to submit support reports. It should submit only
when you ask or agree. Reports go privately to ComingUp, not to a public GitHub
issue. Keep passwords and private household information out of the description.
Submitting a report does not promise a particular fix or new feature.

## Check for a reply

Ask “What's the status of my ComingUp report?” or “Have you received any replies
to my feature request?” With permission to read support reports, the assistant
can show your own reports and replies shared with you. Older connections may
need to reconnect so you can approve support access.

## If your assistant cannot connect

You can always email [support@comingup.today](mailto:support@comingup.today).
Include the feature or assistant you were using, what you expected to happen,
and what happened instead. Never send your password.
